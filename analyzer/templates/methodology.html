{% translate "How to read Chartist" %}

{% translate "Chartist describes recorded voting patterns. It does not measure a party's ideology, explain its motives, or recommend whom to vote for." %}

{% translate "Which votes are included?" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed with start=period_start|date:"j F Y" %}The import includes motions started after {{ start }} that have a voting decision in the Tweede Kamer's open data. Bills and amendments are outside this dataset.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Each party has one recorded position per motion. For roll-call votes, the majority of participating members determines that position. An even split is stored separately from non-participation and counts as participation." %}

{% translate "The dates shown are voting-session dates where available. Monthly comparisons fall back to the record's modification date when the voting date is missing. Official publications remain the source for the motion's wording." %}

{% translate "Parties that continue under another name" %}

{% translate "A successor inherits its predecessor's votes on motions for which it has no record of its own. Its own records take precedence. This preserves the voting history of a continuing party without inventing votes for a new split." %}

{% translate "The predecessor keeps its own history. A predecessor and its successor are not ranked against each other in the agreement matrix. Party pages explain the relationship, and comparisons identify inherited votes." %}

{% translate "How the map is calculated" %}

{% translate "Principal component analysis (PCA) finds patterns across motions, with parties as the observations. A vote for is coded as 1, against as −1, and an even split as 0. Recorded non-participation and missing records stay missing." %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed with threshold=participation_threshold|floatformat:"0" %}The current inclusion rule requires at least {{ threshold }}% recorded-vote coverage from a party's first recorded vote onward, including inherited history. This coverage includes non-participation records. The participation percentage shown on party pages counts for, against and evenly split positions, using the same period. It measures participation at party level, not individual members’ attendance.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed with coverage=fit_coverage|floatformat:"0" %}The axes are fitted using eligible parties with a for, against or evenly split position on at least {{ coverage }}% of all motions. Only motions with such a position for every fitted party enter that fit. Motions are centred, without standardising their variance.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed with minimum=minimum_projected_votes %}Other eligible parties are projected onto those axes using only their for, against or evenly split positions. They need at least {{ minimum }} such positions on the fitted motions. Missing votes are not replaced by an average. A shorter voting history provides less evidence for a party's position.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "What distance and percentages mean" %}

{% translate "Parties close together have similar positions on the displayed voting patterns. The axes use the same scale. A 2D view omits the third axis; the 3D view still leaves out patterns beyond the first three axes." %}

{% translate "An axis's percentage is its share of variance in the fitted parties' voting data. It is not prediction accuracy, a confidence score, or the percentage of motions correctly classified. The combined percentage refers to the three axes together." %}

{% translate "Axis direction has no inherent political meaning. The motions and parties at each end explain the pattern. A motion's weight describes its contribution to an axis, not its importance as public policy." %}

{% translate "How agreement is calculated" %}

{% translate "Agreement is the share of motions on which both parties voted the same, among motions on which both voted for or against. Non-participation, even splits and missing records are excluded. Each motion has equal weight; party seats do not weight this percentage." %}

{% translate "Pairs can cover different periods when parties started or stopped at different times. Check the shared-motion count and voting dates when comparing percentages. Monthly charts leave months with fewer than 50 shared motions empty." %}

{% translate "Voting alike does not establish an alliance or agreement on every topic. The motion lists let you inspect the decisions behind an overall percentage." %}
